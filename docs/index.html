Optimization Modeling

Land Use Optimization Modeling for Sustainable Development

Problem: How can land-use planning balance economic, environmental, and community impacts under competing development priorities?

Methods: Built a three-stage analytical pipeline using OpenCV-style map segmentation, heat maps, erosion/dilation, multi-factor evaluation, AHP, and a genetic algorithm.

Outcome: Produced an optimization framework with sensitivity and generalizability analysis to compare planning scenarios and make tradeoffs more transparent.

Classification

Species Classification for Lizard Biodiversity Analysis

Problem: Can morphometric measurements reliably classify 8 lizard species and sex for biodiversity analysis?

Methods: Compared SVM, AdaBoost, and Random Forest models with 8-fold cross-validation, kernel selection, feature optimization, StandardScaler, and PCA.

Outcome: Reached up to 99.82% accuracy for species isolation and used robustness analysis to evaluate model stability across feature choices.

Forecasting

Climate Change Modeling: CO2 Concentration & Global Temperature Correlation

Problem: How strongly do atmospheric CO2 concentration and global mean surface-air temperature move together, and how can future trends be forecasted?

Methods: Built regression, ARIMA, and BP neural network models, then compared performance with R2 = 0.999, sensitivity analysis, and Pearson/Spearman correlation.

Outcome: Framed climate modeling as both statistical forecasting and climate-risk communication, translating model results into interpretable trend evidence.

Scheduling Optimization

Intelligent Course Scheduling System Using Genetic Algorithm

Problem: How can schools generate course schedules that satisfy hard constraints while improving classroom utilization, walking distance, and timing preferences?

Methods: Started with user interviews with school administrators, modeled hard and soft constraints, and implemented a genetic algorithm from scratch with adaptive crossover/mutation and conflict-resolution logic.

Outcome: Designed a prototype mini-program visualization and outlined a future A/B testing roadmap to evaluate scheduling usability and operational impact.
